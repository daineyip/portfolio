# Binance.US

**Associate Product Manager, Staking Team** · Sept 2023 – Dec 2023 \
**Product Manager Intern, Web3 Team** · Jan 2023 – Aug 2023 · Vancouver, BC

Binance.US is the American arm of the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange. I spent
2023 on the Web3 team, first shipping the launch phase of the self-custody wallet
and then owning fixed-term staking end to end. Every product I launched required delicate
handling of other people's money, while maintaining compliance in a complex regulatory environment.

I led product development for fixed-term staking and launched it to **XXX,XXX+
customers**, unlocking staking opportunities from **10+ tokens** that were not supported prior, 
lifting staking **TVL by XX%.**

The build included multiple third-party integrations: I managed the relationships with the
external teams, understanding their pricing and API libraries, and translated our needs into

## Self-custody HD wallet

I led end-to-end development of a self-custody wallet from inception alongside a senior
PM, owning the product specs and engineering deliverables through to on-time internal
UAT.

I defined the system architecture, evaluated key-custody strategies, and mapped the API
integration flows that connect a wallet to decentralized applications.

I also proposed and delivered the wallet's end-to-end OFAC compliance product, reaching
**100% OFAC coverage across XX,XXX+ daily transactions**. Delivering it meant owning
the third-party vendor integrations, involving pricing and scalability modelling, and weighing 
cost-benefit tradeoffs across multiple API vendors and a proprietary solution.

## WalletConnect

I led a **5-person cross-functional team** as PM to ship the WalletConnect
integration, unlocking transactions across **40,000+ dApps** for wallet users.

I built the connection and signing flows as mockups in **Figma**, and used them
to align designers, engineers and legal teams on a shared solution. From there I integrated 
the **WalletConnect API library** into the wallet, mapping each call to the needs dictated in a strict PRD.

WalletConnect shipped inside **Phase 1 of the Web3 wallet** in an on-time global rollout, after an internal UAT.
