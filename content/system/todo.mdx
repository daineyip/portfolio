The palette is already tokens in `app/globals.css`, so it's mostly a second set of
values. The work is what the tokens don't cover:

- The hard black shadows are the whole visual language, and black-on-dark reads as
  nothing. They need a light shadow colour, not a removed one
- Same problem with the `border-[3px]` black borders
- The wallpaper dot grid needs its own contrast pass
- Follow `prefers-color-scheme`, then let a menu-bar toggle override and stick
