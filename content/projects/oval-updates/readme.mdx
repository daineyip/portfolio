# Oval Updates

**Solo build** · 2026 – present

A single Trump post can move billions in minutes. Oval Updates watches everything he
and the White House put out, from Truth Social posts to press releases, and when something market-relevant lands it sends a Telegram alert
with the affected tickers, a one-line trader's take, an estimated move probability, and
a directional confidence.

## The problem

The posts that matter arrive at all hours, buried in a firehose of ceremonial messages,
political grievances, and rhetoric irrelevant to the market. They're easy to miss and hard to
act on quickly.

## What an alert looks like

```
Alert: Trump threatens to cancel Boeing Air Force One deal
Source: Truth Social
Est. relevance: 73% (company action)
Sentiment: Bearish
Context: Trump attacked Boeing over Air Force One cost overruns and floated
         canceling the contract.
Recommendation: Watch BA for downside on contract-risk headlines; attacks like
                this have historically been short-lived.

Tickers:
• $BA (Boeing) — Directly named; contract-cancellation threat.
```

## Two numbers, not one

**Est. relevance** asks whether the post moves anything at all.
**Directional confidence** asks whether we know which way, given a move. 

## Calibrated on 132 labeled events

I hand-labeled **132 historical Trump and White House events** with what the market
actually did, by category, and derived per-category base rates that anchor the model's
scoring rather than letting it guess.

## The pipeline

```
Truth Social ┐
WH actions   ├─▶ trigger.dev (every 15 min) ─▶ Gemini (2-stage) ─▶ score & gate ─▶ Telegram
WH news      ┘        dedupe, no database        relevance + direction
```

Two stages, for cost as much as accuracy. A **gate** model scores every post and decides
alert-or-skip, tuned for recall so it almost never misses a real mover. The **analyst**
(Gemini 2.5 Pro) runs only on the ~25% that survive, producing the tickers and direction
in the alert body.

**Stack:** TypeScript (ESM, Node 22) · trigger.dev v4 · Google Gemini
(`2.5-flash-lite` gate + `2.5-pro` analyst) · fast-xml-parser · Telegram Bot API.

## Honest limitations

Estimating market direction is hard and unreliable. Oval Updates acts as an alert, rather than a trade signal.
